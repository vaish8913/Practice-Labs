We work with a family-run studio outside Oaxaca that has thrown stoneware for three generations. Every glaze is mixed by hand in small batches, which is why no two pieces — and no two seasons — come out quite the same.

We keep the collection small on purpose. Fewer pieces, made properly, replace more pieces made carelessly.
